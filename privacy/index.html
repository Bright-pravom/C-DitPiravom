Privacy Policy

C-Dit Piravom is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly.

1. Introduction

This policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, contact us, or enquire about our courses.

2. Information Collected

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, phone number, email address, and enquiry details submitted through the website.

3. How Information Is Used

We use this information to respond to enquiries, provide course guidance, and support communication with learners and visitors.

4. Contact / Enquiry Data

Any personal information collected through forms or direct contact is used only for the purpose stated in the enquiry and for appropriate follow-up communication.

5. Cookies

Our website may use limited cookies or session data to improve functionality and provide a smoother browsing experience. We do not use cookies for intrusive tracking unless clearly stated.

6. Analytics

We may use basic website analytics to understand user traffic and improve the website experience, but only if such analytics tools are enabled and configured.

7. Third-Party Services

We may rely on third-party services such as email or communication tools to process enquiries. We do not share personal information beyond what is necessary to respond to a request.

8. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information only as long as needed to respond to the request, maintain records, or comply with legal or administrative obligations.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

10. User Rights

Visitors may request information about the personal data held for them, ask for corrections, or request that their data not be used for a specific purpose by contacting the institute directly.

11. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children without appropriate guidance and parental consent when required by law.

12. Policy Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with the updated date if relevant.

13. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your information is handled, please contact C-Dit Piravom using the details on the contact page.
